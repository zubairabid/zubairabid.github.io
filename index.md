# Hey, I'm Zubair, and I like Computers

A GW-BASIC addition-program on my cousin's laptop piqued my interest back
when I was 12, and the fascination has held me ever since. In the decade
that followed, through toy-projects at school, an enduring love-affair
with Linux, various courses in my CS
program at college, internships, and finally my job, I figured out that
what I really loved was problem solving and extensive technical
challenges. 

I'm a Software Engineer with 4 years of experience in the industry. My
professional experience is in cloud computing, covering various verticals
from client-side command line interfaces to distributed systems at
enterprise scale. I currently work in the 
[Azure Backup](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/backup/backup-overview)
team at [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-in).

[Resume](./_config.ymlZubair_2026_Resume.pdf)

I received my Bachelor's in Computer Science from the
[International  Institute of Information Technology (IIIT-H)](https://www.iiit.ac.in/).
While at the institute, I TA-ed two courses - "Introduction
to Literature" and "Literature, History and Belonging in Hyderabad",
held the position of my Institute's _Student Web Admin_ for a year and
a half, and worked in the Machine Translation and Natural Language
Processing Lab for a hot [minute](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DazYV3EAAAAJ&hl=en).
For two years, I was the
_Editor-in-Chief_ for [Ping!](https://pingiiit.org/), and was also
involved as _Head of Tech_ and _Head of Events_ for the
[Entrepreneurship Cell](https://ecell.iiit.ac.in/).
